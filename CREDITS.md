# Credits

Everything scanned is from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com), released under
CC0 (public domain). Attribution is not required; it is given here as a courtesy.

- Trees and snags: `fir_tree_01`, `pine_tree_01`, `fir_sapling_medium`,
  `pine_sapling_medium`. The scene ships pictures rendered from them
  (`data/cards_*.webp`), their trunk and branch meshes in reduced form, and
  their bark and sprig textures (`data/near_*`), not the full models.
- Meadow plants, as rendered pictures (`data/plants.webp`): `grass_medium_01`,
  `grass_medium_02`, `dandelion_01`, `periwinkle_plant`, `shrub_01`.
- Sedge, rush, corn lily and willow are not scans: they are modelled in
  `tools/proc_plants.js` for this project. The clumps of plants seen from the
  air are drawn in `src/rosettes.js`.
- Logs, in reduced form (`data/logs.bin`, `data/log_*.webp`): `dead_tree_trunk`,
  `dead_tree_trunk_02`.
- Ground textures (`data/ground_*.webp`): `forrest_ground_03`,
  `river_small_rocks`, `dirt_floor`.

three.js is MIT licensed (see `vendor/three/LICENSE`).

Terrain: USGS 3DEP 1 m lidar DEM (public domain), processed with ERASER.
